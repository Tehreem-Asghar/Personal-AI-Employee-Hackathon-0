# 👋 Welcome to your Digital FTE (Full-Time Equivalent)

> *"Your life and business on autopilot. Local-first, agent-driven, human-in-the-loop."*

This Obsidian vault serves as the **Memory** and **GUI** (Graphical User Interface) for your Personal AI Employee. Unlike traditional automation, this system is proactive—it watches your world and prepares work for your approval.

---

## 🏗️ How it Works

The system is built on four pillars:
1. **The Brain**: Gemini 2.0 Flash (via Drafting Agent) processes tasks and drafts replies.
2. **The Senses**: Watchers monitor **Gmail**, **WhatsApp**, **LinkedIn**, and your **File System**.
3. **The Hands**: Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Playwright automation send emails and post to social media.
4. **The Memory**: This vault, where every task is tracked as a Markdown file.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

1. **Monitor**: Open the [[Dashboard]] to see live system status.
2. **Review**: Check [[Pending_Approval]] for drafts prepared by the AI.
3. **Execute**: Move any file from `/Pending_Approval` to [[Approved]] to send it live.
4. **Learn**: Read the [[Company_Handbook]] to understand the rules of engagement.

---

## 🛠️ Management via CLI
You can use Claude CLI to interact with the vault using specialized skills:
- `vault-audit`: Get a high-level summary of all folders.
- `social-expert`: Draft creative LinkedIn content.
- `briefing-genius`: Generate a summary of completed work.
