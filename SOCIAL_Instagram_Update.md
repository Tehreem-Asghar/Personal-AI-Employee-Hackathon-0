Write a high-engagement Instagram post about building consistency in learning tech skills.

Target Audience: Students and beginner developers  
Tone: Motivational, relatable, and slightly emotional  
Length: 8–12 short lines (easy to read)

Structure:
- Start with a powerful hook (something that grabs attention instantly)
- Talk about the struggle of staying consistent
- Share a small turning point or mindset shift
- Give 1 practical tip for staying consistent
- End with a strong call to action (e.g., “Comment CONSISTENT if you're committed!”)

Include:
- Natural emojis (not too many)
- 6–8 relevant hashtags
- Make it scroll-stopping and inspiring
